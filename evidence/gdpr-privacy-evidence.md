# GDPR Privacy Compliance Evidence

## Purpose

This folder contains supporting evidence for the simulated GDPR Privacy and Data Protection Compliance Assessment for CloudNova Technologies.

The evidence demonstrates how personal data processing, lawful basis, privacy risks, data subject rights, and remediation activities were reviewed and documented.

## Evidence Included

- GDPR control matrix
- Personal data inventory
- Lawful basis review
- GDPR gap assessment
- Privacy risk register
- Data subject rights review
- Remediation plan

## Key Privacy Areas Reviewed

- Privacy Governance
- Personal Data Inventory
- Records of Processing Activities
- Lawful Basis
- Consent Management
- Data Minimization
- Data Retention
- Data Subject Rights
- Processor Management
- International Data Transfers
- Privacy Breach Response
- DPIA
- Accountability

## Final Assessment

The simulated assessment identified High and Medium privacy risks requiring remediation to strengthen GDPR accountability and data protection practices.
